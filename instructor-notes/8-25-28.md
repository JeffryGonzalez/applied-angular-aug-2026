## Quick Review

- "Appled Angular"
  - I don't know how you will apply Angular, but I know *where* you will
    - Angular is the easy part. Really. Especially with AI. But:
      - Make your AI venue aware.
      - Don't just hand your AI a story card. My suggestion:
        - Share the objective. What needs done.
        - Describe that affordance - how will we provide this - without venue concerns.
        - Discuss how to provide that within the venue constraints. (State, auth, bundling, A11Y, etc.)
        - The "staging" of your affordance will be "multi-dimensional", representing all the venues.
        - Components are the worse place for that. They are *pure* venue adapter.
    - "Teaching would be easy if it wasn't for the kids" - my dad.
    - Angular is glue that glues a user to a bunch of venues and tries to hide it from them
    - Angular apps live right up against the user - right behind the glass. If you can't hide the venue friction, is a server-side web application.
